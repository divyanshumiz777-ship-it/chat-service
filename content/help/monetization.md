# Tips and creator tiers

Creators can earn on TheSocial in two ways. Payments are handled through
Razorpay.

## Tips

A tip is a one-off payment to a creator. Tipping only appears for creators who
have turned tips on for their account; if you do not see the option, that
creator has not enabled it.

## Enabling tips

Open Settings and turn tips on in the payouts section. Until you do, no tip
button is shown on your profile.

## Community tiers

A community owner can offer paid tiers that members subscribe to. A subscription
is recurring, unlike a tip. Tier subscriptions have a status — active, past due,
or cancelled — and only active ones grant tier benefits.
