# Communities

A community (also called a server) is a space you create or join to talk with a
group of people. Every community contains categories, and each category holds
channels.

## Creating a community

Use the **+** button in the top navigation bar and give the community a name and
an optional image. A new community starts with three text channels already set
up for you: **notes**, **social-zone**, and **doubts**. You become the owner.

## Joining a community

Open **Discover** to browse public communities and join one directly. You can
also join through an invite link shared by an existing member. Private
communities are only reachable by invite.

## Public vs private

A community is either public or private. Public communities appear in Discover
and their content can surface in search for anyone. Private communities are
visible only to their members, and their channels, messages and voice recaps
stay restricted to members.

## Leaving a community

Open the community and choose to leave it. Leaving removes your access to its
channels; it does not delete anything you posted.
