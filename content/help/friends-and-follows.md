# Friends and follows

TheSocial has two different relationships, and they work differently.

## Friends — mutual

A friendship must be accepted by both people. You send a friend request; it
stays pending until the other person accepts it. Being friends lets you message
each other directly and see more of each other's profile.

## Follows — one-way

Following a creator is one-way and does not need to be mutual: their reels start
appearing in your feed. A creator with a private profile may need to approve the
follow before it takes effect.

## Nicknames

You can set your own private nickname for a friend. Only you see it.

## Finding people

Search for people by name, or ask the assistant to suggest friends — it looks at
mutual friends, communities you share, and overlapping interests.
