# Notifications

You are notified about mentions, friend requests, follows, replies, and activity
in communities you belong to.

## Controlling notifications

Settings → Notifications lets you choose how much you receive, and turn email or
push notifications on and off. Individual communities and conversations can be
muted separately, so a busy community can be silenced without affecting the rest.

## Catching up

If you have been away, ask the assistant "what did I miss" for a summary of
activity across your communities and direct messages since you were last around.
