# Getting started

TheSocial is a community platform. The pieces fit together like this:

- **Communities** are groups you join. Each contains **channels** where
  conversation happens.
- **Direct messages** are private conversations with one person or a small group.
- **Reels** are short videos, with their own feed.
- **Friends** are mutual connections; **follows** are one-way, for creators
  whose reels you want to see.

## First steps

1. Open **Discover** and join a community that interests you, or create your own.
2. Say hello in one of its channels.
3. Add a profile picture and a short bio in Settings.
4. Follow a few creators so your reels feed has something in it.
5. Consider turning on two-factor authentication to secure your account.

## Getting help

Ask the assistant. It can explain how any part of TheSocial works, and show you
your own activity.
