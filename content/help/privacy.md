# Privacy and blocking

## Profile visibility

Your profile is one of three settings:

- **Public** — anyone can view it, and it can appear in search.
- **Friends** — only people you are friends with can see the full profile.
- **Private** — hidden from everyone else and never surfaced in search.

## Direct message requests

"Allow DM requests" controls whether people who are not your friends can start a
conversation with you. Turn it off to restrict direct messages to friends.

## Blocking

Blocking someone stops their messages reaching you, and their content stops
appearing in your search results and assistant answers. Blocking works in both
directions: you also stop appearing to them.

## What the assistant can see

The assistant only ever sees what you can already see. It cannot show you a
private community you are not a member of, a private profile, or a conversation
you are not part of, and it respects blocks.
