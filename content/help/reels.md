# Reels

Reels are short videos. The reels feed shows videos chosen for you based on the
creators you follow, the tags you engage with, and what is currently popular.

## Posting a reel

Upload a video and add a caption and tags. Tags matter: they are how your reel
is matched to people interested in that subject.

## Captions

Subtitles are generated automatically after upload, so a reel can be watched
without sound. Captions may take a moment to become ready after posting.

## Public and private reels

A reel is either public or private. Public reels can be discovered by anyone and
can appear in search; private ones are not surfaced.

## Interacting

You can like, comment on, share, and save a reel. Saved reels are collected in
your Saved items for later. Sharing produces a link that works for anyone who
opens it.
