# Search

Search covers communities, channels, threads, people, reels, messages and voice
call recaps, and only ever returns things you already have access to.

## Where to search

Open the assistant and switch to the **Search** tab. Filter by type to narrow
results to, say, only reels or only people. From the assistant's Chat tab you can
also scope a search directly with a command such as `/search reels` followed by
what you are looking for.

## How it works

Search matches meaning, not just exact words, so a query like "video editing"
can find a community about filmmaking even when those exact words do not appear.
Results are ranked by how well they match and how recent they are.

## Saved items

Separately from search, you can save individual messages and reels. Saved items
are collected on their own page for later.
