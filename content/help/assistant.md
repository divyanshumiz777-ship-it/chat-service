# The assistant

The assistant helps you with TheSocial: your own activity, the people and
communities on it, and how the product works. It is not a general-purpose
chatbot and will not answer questions about the outside world.

## Asking for things

Type naturally ("suggest communities", "what did I miss"), or press **@** in the
message box to open the command list and pick one. Commands starting with **/**
run a specific capability directly.

## What it can do

- Show your friends, communities, followed creators and recent conversations.
- Suggest communities, friends and creators to discover.
- Summarize a community, a channel, or a conversation.
- Recap what was said in a voice call.
- Search across communities, people, reels, messages and calls.
- Answer questions about how TheSocial works.
- Tell you what you missed while you were away.

## Tabs

**Chat** is the conversation. **Search** searches everything you have access to.
**Digest** shows a summary of recent activity.

## What it cannot do

It cannot act for you — it will not send messages, join communities or follow
people on your behalf. It also cannot see anything you cannot see yourself.
