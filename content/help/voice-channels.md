# Voice and video channels

Joining a Voice or Video channel connects you to everyone else currently in it.

## Live captions

Voice and video channels can show live captions of what is being said,
generated automatically as people speak. Captions are off unless a community
admin turns transcription on for that channel, and participants are shown a
notice when it is active.

## Call recaps

When a voice session ends, TheSocial can produce a written recap of it —
a short summary, the key points raised, and any action items. The recap is
saved with the channel so you can read what was discussed if you missed the
call, and you can ask the assistant about it later.

## Direct calls

You can also start a one-to-one voice or video call with a friend from a direct
message conversation, separately from community voice channels.
