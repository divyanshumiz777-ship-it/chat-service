# Direct messages and groups

## Direct messages

A direct message is a private one-to-one conversation. You can start one with a
friend, or with anyone who allows direct message requests. Whether strangers can
message you is controlled by the "Allow DM requests" setting in Privacy.

## Group conversations

A group brings several people into one private conversation. Any participant can
leave; the owner can manage membership.

## What you can send

Text, images, video, audio, documents and voice notes. Several files can be sent
at once, and consecutive photos are grouped into a single album. Links become
clickable automatically.

## Managing a conversation

You can react to a message with an emoji, reply to it, forward it elsewhere, pin
it, edit your own messages, or delete them. Deleting has two forms: **for me**,
which hides the message only from you, and **for everyone**, which removes it
for all participants.

## Hiding and blocking

A conversation can be hidden without deleting it. Blocking someone stops their
messages reaching you and removes their content from your search results.
