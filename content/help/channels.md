# Channels and threads

Channels live inside a community's categories and are where conversation
happens. A channel is one of three types:

- **Text** — ordinary messages, files, images and voice notes.
- **Voice** — live audio conversation.
- **Video** — live audio and video.

## Creating a channel

Community owners, admins and moderators can create channels. Pick the category
it belongs to and the channel type. Ordinary members cannot create channels.

## Threads

A thread is a side conversation branched off a channel, useful for keeping a
long discussion from taking over the main channel. Threads inherit the
community's privacy: a thread in a private community stays private.

## Pinned messages

Any message can be pinned in a channel so it stays easy to find. Pinned
messages appear in a bar at the top of the channel.
